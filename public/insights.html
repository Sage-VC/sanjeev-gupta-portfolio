Scenario Reflection

Career Transitions: A Typical Coaching Scenario

Sometimes the leadership question becomes a career question.

You may be moving into a larger role, considering a lateral move, dealing with changed responsibilities, questioning future growth or thinking about leaving your organisation. Sometimes the change may not be entirely your choice.

And after a long corporate career, the question may simply become: “What do I want the next phase of my life to look like?”

At senior levels, these decisions are rarely only about finding another job. They can involve questions about priorities, identity, ambition, relevance, risk and what success means to you now.

I have navigated a significant professional transition myself — from global operations leadership into advisory, consulting and executive coaching. Your journey will be different from mine, but I understand why these decisions often need more thought than they first appear to.
