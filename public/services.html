Service Offerings • Tailored Engagement Models

Different Situations Need Different Kinds of Support

A leader dealing with a difficult situation may need space to think and reflect. An organisation looking to strengthen its leaders may need a focused development intervention. A business facing execution challenges may need an experienced external perspective.

Three Practice Areas

My Work Spans Three Core Areas

Each offering is structured to address challenges at the individual, team, or systemic operational level.

01

Executive Coaching

Think clearly. Lead differently.

Executive coaching provides leaders with a safe, confidential and non-judgemental space to step away from immediate pressures and look at situations differently.

Useful when dealing with larger roles, team/stakeholder challenges, constant firefighting, difficulty delegating, emotional reactions under pressure, resilience, confidence, change or career transitions.

Decision Guidance

Which Approach Is Right?

Leadership and execution are closely connected. Sometimes an execution challenge reveals a leadership capability gap. At other times, organisational structures or ways of working prevent leaders from applying what they have learnt.

Coaching helps leaders reflect, create awareness and discover their own answers. Leadership Development builds the capability of leaders to handle challenges more effectively. Advisory brings experience and an external perspective to a business or organisational challenge.

Sometimes more than one approach may be useful.

The method should follow the need — not the other way around.

You don't need to choose a service before contacting me. Tell me what is happening, what you would like to be different and what seems to be getting in the way.
